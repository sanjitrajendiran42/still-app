QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Before you begin.

Is Still a medical treatment?+

No. Still is a wellness and behavior-change tool, not medical care, diagnosis, detox or treatment. Alcohol withdrawal can be dangerous; people who may be physically dependent should seek medical guidance before stopping suddenly.

Does Still send my recovery data anywhere?+

The iOS launch version stores your recovery plan, journal and check-ins locally on your device using iOS file protection. It does not include ad trackers or third-party analytics SDKs.

Can I use Still to cut back instead of quit?+

Yes. During onboarding you can choose to quit, take a clear break or regain control. Still adapts the language of your plan to the goal you choose.

When is Android coming?+

We are launching on iPhone first so we can make the core experience exceptional. Google Play is the next platform on the roadmap.

What happens if I have a lapse?+

Still treats a lapse as information, not the erasure of your progress. You can record what happened, reset the current streak and keep the lessons and history you already built.
